Built for Hawaii

Why island projects need a different approach

☀

Intense UV

Year-round sun breaks down binders and fades color. We specify UV-stable, high-solids systems.

≈

Salt Air

Airborne salt accelerates corrosion and adhesion failure. Proper prep and primers are essential.

☂

Humidity & Rain

Moisture and mildew require breathable, mildew-resistant coatings and careful scheduling.
