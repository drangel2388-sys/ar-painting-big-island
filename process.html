A&R Painting

A proven process, from first call to final walkthrough.

Five clear steps that keep large projects on schedule, on budget, and up to standard.

1
Step 1

Consultation

We meet on site or virtually to understand your goals, timeline, budget, and any constraints like occupancy, HOA rules, or GC schedules.

  • Site walk & condition assessment
  • Discuss scope, colors & performance needs
  • Identify access, safety & logistics
2
Step 2

Detailed Estimate

You receive a clear, itemized proposal — no vague lump sums — with product specifications and a proposed schedule.

  • Itemized scope & pricing
  • Coating system & product data
  • Schedule, phasing & warranty terms
3
Step 3

Preparation

Prep is where quality is won. We protect your property and prepare surfaces properly for long-lasting adhesion.

  • Protection of floors, landscaping & fixtures
  • Pressure washing, scraping & sanding
  • Repairs to stucco, wood, drywall & metal
  • Priming & caulking
4
Step 4

Painting & Finishing

Skilled crews apply the specified system with precise technique, under the oversight of a dedicated project manager.

  • Manufacturer-recommended application
  • Daily clean-up & secure job site
  • Regular progress updates
5
Step 5

Final Walkthrough

We walk the project with you, address every punch-list item, and hand over documentation and warranty.

  • Detailed walkthrough & punch list
  • Touch-ups & final clean
  • Color records, product data & warranty
Project Management

Communication you can count on.

Every project is assigned a dedicated project manager who serves as your single point of contact. Expect clear schedules, proactive updates, and fast answers.

  • Pre-construction meeting & written schedule
  • Weekly progress reports [adjust to your practice]
  • Safety plan & site-specific protocols
  • Closeout package with colors, products & warranty
